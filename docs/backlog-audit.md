## Confirmed Documentation Mismatches

- `docs/backend-design.md:233` says general per-IP/public-read limits are not implemented, while `server/rate-limits.ts` and `docs/deployment.md:41` show in-memory per-process limits are implemented. Draft #12 includes reconciling this wording with shared-store behavior.
- `docs/deployment.md:3` says the project has not been deployed; `docs/demo-deployment.md:3-5` documents the live demo. Draft #26 will distinguish a live demo from production qualification.

## Unconfirmed and Excluded

- The actual PostgreSQL network allowlist, hosted backup schedule, restore capability, and database account grants were not inspected. Drafts #13-15 and #25 specify verification/controls; they do not claim those hosted settings are currently misconfigured.
- Whether operators want custom organization names, bookmarkable filters, or a full beneficiary filter set is a product decision; code confirms those capabilities are absent, not their adoption priority.
- No production browser journey, real-world beneficiary consent evidence, or chain transaction outcome was independently verified in this source audit.
- Candidate overlaps deliberately left out include CSV formula-safety work (near #12), activity-list pagination (near #22), scheduled reverification (#18), and any accessibility/localization or E2E expansion (#21, #23, #24).
