# Community Standards

AidTrace is a small open-source project. Everyone participating in its issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions is expected to help keep those spaces respectful and useful.

Participants should communicate in good faith, critique ideas rather than people, respect different backgrounds and experience levels, and make room for questions and corrections. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

If something makes participation unsafe or unproductive, report it privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed as capacity allows. The project may edit or remove harmful contributions or restrict participation when necessary. Retaliation against a person who raises a concern is not acceptable.

This document applies to project spaces and to conduct that materially affects participation in them.
